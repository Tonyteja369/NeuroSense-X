# Code of Conduct

## Cranial Mechanical Resonance Mapping (CMRM)

### 1. Our Commitment

The Cranial Mechanical Resonance Mapping (CMRM) project is committed to providing an open, respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for everyone participating in its development and research.

We welcome contributions from individuals with different backgrounds, experiences, technical abilities, and areas of expertise, including:

* Biomedical Engineering
* Electronics and Embedded Systems
* Mechanical Engineering
* Signal Processing
* Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
* Software Development
* Scientific Research

We believe that respectful collaboration, scientific integrity, and responsible engineering are essential to meaningful research.

### 2. Expected Behaviour

All project participants are expected to:

* Treat everyone with respect, professionalism, and courtesy.
* Communicate constructively and respectfully.
* Welcome different technical perspectives and research approaches.
* Provide constructive feedback.
* Accept reasonable criticism and suggestions.
* Respect the contributions and intellectual efforts of others.
* Share knowledge and support collaborative learning.
* Maintain scientific honesty and research integrity.
* Document technical changes clearly.
* Respect project guidelines and community decisions.

### 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
* Personal attacks or insulting language.
* Bullying, threats, or deliberate humiliation.
* Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Deliberately disrupting project discussions.
* Misrepresenting another contributor's work.
* Plagiarism or presenting someone else's research as original work.
* Fabricating experimental measurements or research results.
* Knowingly presenting unvalidated findings as established scientific evidence.
* Making unsupported medical or diagnostic claims about CMRM.

### 4. Scientific Integrity and Research Responsibility

CMRM is an experimental biomedical engineering research project. All contributors must maintain responsible scientific and engineering practices.

Contributors are expected to:

1. Report experimental results honestly.
2. Distinguish measured data from simulated or synthetic data.
3. Document experimental configurations and processing parameters.
4. Avoid manipulating results to support predetermined conclusions.
5. Clearly identify limitations and uncertainties.
6. Reference external research and technical resources appropriately.
7. Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.
8. Avoid unsupported clinical or medical claims.
9. Protect sensitive research information.
10. Report significant technical or experimental errors transparently.

CMRM must not be represented as a clinically validated diagnostic system unless appropriate evidence and regulatory requirements have been established.

### 5. Responsible Engineering

Contributors working on hardware, firmware, software, and experimental procedures should prioritize responsible engineering.

This includes:

* Following appropriate electrical safety practices.
* Verifying power-management circuits before operation.
* Testing hardware under controlled conditions.
* Avoiding unsafe experimental configurations.
* Documenting hardware limitations.
* Protecting experimental data.
* Reviewing potentially unsafe software or firmware changes.
* Using appropriate non-human mechanical phantoms during initial research and development.

Any proposed human-subject research must undergo the appropriate ethical review, consent, and safety procedures.

### 6. Collaboration Guidelines

To maintain a productive development environment:

* Use clear and descriptive GitHub issues.
* Submit focused pull requests.
* Explain the purpose of proposed changes.
* Include relevant testing information.
* Document new hardware and software dependencies.
* Avoid unnecessary conflicts in technical discussions.
* Respect project maintainers and review processes.
* Give appropriate credit to contributors.

Technical disagreements should be resolved through evidence, respectful discussion, and reproducible experimentation.

### 7. Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies to all CMRM project spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories.
* Issues and pull requests.
* Discussions.
* Project documentation.
* Research collaboration.
* Official project communication channels.
* Events or activities conducted on behalf of the project.

It also applies when an individual's behaviour in another setting has a reasonable impact on the safety or well-being of the project community.

### 8. Reporting Unacceptable Behaviour

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers.

Reports should include, where possible:

* A description of the incident.
* Relevant dates and circumstances.
* Links, screenshots, or other supporting information.
* Any additional context that may help assess the situation.

Reports will be reviewed with appropriate confidentiality.

### 9. Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the seriousness and circumstances of the behaviour, possible actions include:

1. A private warning.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal of inappropriate content.
5. Temporary suspension from project spaces.
6. Permanent removal from project participation.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and based on the available information.

### 10. Confidentiality

Project maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of individuals who report incidents.

Information related to reports will be shared only when necessary to review or address the situation.

### 11. Acknowledgements

This Code of Conduct is informed by established open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.

The project-specific provisions additionally address scientific integrity, experimental safety, research reproducibility, and responsible biomedical engineering development.

### 12. Commitment

By participating in the Cranial Mechanical Resonance Mapping project, contributors agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, responsible, and scientifically honest research community.
